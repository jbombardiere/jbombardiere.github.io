Mercer County Science Fair

Automatic kill switch Science fair - 3rd place

I was a part of the Mercer county science and engineering fair in Princeton University. Following the distortion pedal I decided to create an automatic killswitch guitar pedal. The pedal periodically cuts the guitar signal to make a stuttering kind of effect. I received 3rd place in the technology enhancing arts category. Overall this was a very fun experience and let me show and explain my project to a lot of other people.
